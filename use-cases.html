02 Stage II — silent speech

Speak without a sound.

The necklace reads speech-muscle signals. You articulate — silently, no sound at all — and VOIZEX turns it into your natural voice. Three modes, one device. 92% accuracy in our prototype today.

i.

Voice output

You articulate with the muscles, no sound — and your cloned voice comes out through the speaker, in the chosen language.

ii.

Soundless dialogue

You articulate silently; a connected partner hears you through an earpiece in their chosen language. A private channel in a public room.

iii.

Speechless commands

You articulate a command — and paired devices or systems carry it out. A human–machine interface with no wake word said aloud.
